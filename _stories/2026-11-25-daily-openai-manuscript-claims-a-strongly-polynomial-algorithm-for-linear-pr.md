  "Interior-point and ellipsoid methods solve linear programs in time polynomial in the bit length of the input, but whether a method exists whose number of arithmetic steps depends only on the numbers of variables and constraints has been open; Stephen Smale listed it as the ninth of his problems for the 21st century. A manuscript posted by OpenAI on 24 November claims a deterministic algorithm with that property for arbitrary real coefficients, with intermediate numbers of polynomial bit length on rational input. It returns an optimal primal-dual pair, an improving ray or a certificate of infeasibility, and extends to convex quadratic programming and mean-payoff games.",
  "A companion paper claims that the shortest edge path between two vertices of a bounded simple rational polytope can be superpolynomially long. The Lean formalisation covers feasibility and optimisation for rational data on a fixed model of computation. Neither paper has been peer reviewed."
