  "The paper studies classes of functions that map inputs to probability distributions over a finite label set. Labels are drawn from the conditional distribution of an unknown target in the class, and learning is measured by excess risk under a bounded proper loss satisfying two mild conditions.",
  "The authors define the fat-\\(\\gamma\\) Natarajan dimension, which generalises both the fat-shattering and Natarajan dimensions, and prove that its finiteness at every \\(\\gamma > 0\\) is necessary and sufficient for learnability. A fat-\\(\\gamma\\) graph dimension gives an equivalent characterisation, and a class is learnable if and only if each coordinate class is learnable in the probabilistic concept model. This extends the classical combinatorial theory of PAC learning to probabilistic prediction."
