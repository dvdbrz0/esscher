  "The same guarantee is proved for the full adaptive moment method under an explicit momentum-alignment condition, and the analysis is extended to projected and proximal updates through a subdifferential-distance stationarity measure, to objectives satisfying the Polyak–Łojasiewicz condition, and to heavy-tailed noise through truncation."
