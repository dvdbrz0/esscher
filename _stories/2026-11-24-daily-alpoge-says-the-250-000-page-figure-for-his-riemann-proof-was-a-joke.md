  "Levent Alpöge, the Anthropic mathematician who released a Lean library on 20 November that he said proves the Riemann hypothesis, wrote on X that the widely repeated figure of more than 250,000 pages for a written version was a joke. He said the Lean code is full of garbage, that he had been taking many shortcuts in it, and that criticism of its state was reasonable. The code compiles and is accepted by Lean’s kernel.",
  "The remark separates two things that coverage had run together. Independent checkers had already certified that the library proves Mathlib’s statement of the hypothesis using only Lean’s standard axioms. What no one has is a readable argument, and Alpöge’s description of the code suggests that producing one will take substantial work. Developers have begun stripping redundant files from the repository, and critics, among them Gary Marcus, cited the post against those who had praised a proof they had not read."
