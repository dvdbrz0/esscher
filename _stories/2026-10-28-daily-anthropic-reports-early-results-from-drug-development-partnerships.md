  "At Generate Biomedicines, scientists using Claude Code proposed a version of one component of a CAR-T therapy about 70% shorter than the standard one; in lab tests it let immune cells make roughly 10 times as many receptors. For Divergence Labs, Claude generated roughly 35,000 antibody designs in about a month for a food-allergy programme. BigHat made and tested about 1,010 variants proposed with Claude in about 10 weeks, and the best bound more than 10 times more tightly than the originals. At Enveda, Claude caught all 11 issues human reviewers found in 1,061 pages of manufacturing records, flagged seven more, and cut review time by 89%.",
