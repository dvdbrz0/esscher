  "Alex Imas, Andrew Koh and Shane Legg, a co-founder of Google DeepMind, separate two capabilities of an agent: competence, which shifts a firm’s productivity, and coordination, which governs how productivity scales with size. AI expertise can be copied while human expertise is in fixed supply, and AI may not face human limits on coordination.",
  "In equilibrium, firms of complementary humans can still beat AI that is more capable than the average person. Better AI competence can shrink firms and change who works in them, while better AI coordination can produce a sudden shift to large AI-heavy firms. Employment changes gradually when AI replaces workers inside mixed firms and abruptly when AI firms outcompete human ones, and the authors split wage changes into a technology effect and a reorganisation effect that can reverse it."
