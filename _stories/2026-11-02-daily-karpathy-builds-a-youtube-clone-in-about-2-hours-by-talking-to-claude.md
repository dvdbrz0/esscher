  "Andrej Karpathy, a co-founder of OpenAI who joined Anthropic in May, wrote on Saturday that he had built HackerTube, a video site, in about two hours. He gave Anthropic’s Opus 5.5 model one task: write a YouTube clone from the videos recommended on Hacker News. The model downloaded the site’s comments, ran a cheaper model over them, removed spam and kept 40,000 comments that recommend a video. The result is 6,454 videos from 2007 to 2026 across 150 topics, with midpoint frames in place of uploaded thumbnails and recommendations stored in the browser, without accounts.",
