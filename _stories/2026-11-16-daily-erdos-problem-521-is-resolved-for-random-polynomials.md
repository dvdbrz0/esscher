  "Erdős problem 521 concerns whether \\(N_n/\\log n\\), the normalised number of real roots of a polynomial with independent random signs as coefficients, converges almost surely. Chen et al. show that it does not.",
  "For Kac polynomials \\(P_n(x)=\\sum_{j=0}^n \\xi_j x^j\\) with i.i.d. coefficients of mean zero, variance one and a finite exponential moment, they prove that almost surely \\[ \\liminf_{n\\to\\infty} \\frac{N_n}{\\log n} = \\frac{1}{\\pi}, \\qquad \\limsup_{n\\to\\infty} \\frac{N_n}{\\log n} = \\frac{1}{\\pi} + C_*, \\] where \\(C_* > 1/\\pi\\) is fixed by an equation, and that the set of subsequential limits is exactly \\([1/\\pi, 1/\\pi + C_*]\\). The lower limit holds under only a \\(2+\\delta\\) moment."
