  "Optimised certainty equivalents are a family of risk measures used in portfolio optimisation and risk management, containing the mean-variance criterion, expected shortfall, the entropic risk measure and plain expectation. They had lacked an economic axiomatisation.",
  "The authors introduce Fair-Coin Compensation, based on assigning two losses by a coin toss and asking whether transfers can remove the extra risk. With further axioms they recover sharper classes: monotonicity gives standard certainty equivalents, positive homogeneity with prudence gives expected shortfall, and a fair-coin independence axiom gives the entropic risk measure."
