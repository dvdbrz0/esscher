  "Chollet made the argument while endorsing Gakku, a new company building a conversational AI learning coach that has raised funding. His point is about what such systems are optimised for. A general assistant is judged by whether its answer is right, and a student who uses it that way receives the result without doing the reasoning that produces it.",
  "In his view, the skill a learner is meant to acquire lives in that reasoning, so a tool that outsources it shortcuts the learning itself. An educational system should instead be built around the learner’s own thinking: prompting, questioning and guiding rather than solving. The post did not give evidence or figures; it is a statement of design principle.",
  "The argument fits a wider concern among practitioners that fluent assistants make it easy to produce correct work without understanding it, a problem that matters most where the goal is the skill rather than the output."
