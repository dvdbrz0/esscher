  "Rough volatility models write log-volatility as a Gaussian Volterra process, so the realised variance over a short period is the time integral of an exponential of such a process. The paper proves a small-time large deviation principle for this normalised integral, and complements it with a central limit theorem and moderate deviations.",
  "The application is to variance options. The authors derive short-maturity asymptotics for out-of-the-money and at-the-money variance options in rough volatility models, together with expansions of their implied volatility, and check them numerically. Such formulas give closed-form approximations where pricing otherwise needs simulation."
