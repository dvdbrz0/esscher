  "Fable 5.2 and Mythos 5.2 are the same underlying model. Fable carries safeguards against cybersecurity and biology misuse and is available to all users; Mythos, with lighter restrictions, goes to organisations admitted to Anthropic’s cyber and life-sciences verification programmes. The company said the models generate output up to 30% faster than the 5.1 versions and cost $8 per million input tokens and $40 per million output tokens.",
  "On FrontierCode 2.0, which asks whether a model’s code changes would be merged by the maintainers of open-source projects, Anthropic reported 49.1% for Fable 5.2, against 45.5% for Opus 5.5 and 42.8% for OpenAI’s GPT-6 Astra. On Humanity’s Last Exam with tools it reported 74.2%. Artificial Analysis, which runs its own tests, placed Fable 5.2 first of 74 models on its intelligence index with a score of 65, and also among the slowest and most expensive per task.",
  "The result drawing most attention is mathematical. Anthropic said a team of about 1,000 Fable 5.2 agents fully formalised the proof of Fermat’s Last Theorem in Lean in 30 hours, starting from minimal prompts and a list of intermediate theorem statements, with no mathematical references. Its first formalisation, by an unreleased research model with a purpose-built harness and human guidance, took 11 days; the new proof is seven times shorter, and Opus 5.5 in the same setup completed 26% in 18 hours. The company also said the model settled two open problems about black holes in holographic duality, described in two preprints. None of these claims has yet been independently reviewed."
