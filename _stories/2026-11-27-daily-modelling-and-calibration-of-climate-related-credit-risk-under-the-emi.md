 "body": [
  "Emissions trading systems price a growing share of global emissions, and some caps are due to reach zero within the life of corporate debt. In the model, firms choose emissions from several energy sources given an anticipated carbon price, while the realised price clears the carbon market of each sector and region under a scenario-dependent demand shock, with physical losses reduced by adaptation.",
  "The authors show that an equilibrium carbon price exists, characterise when the cap binds and derive each firm’s optimal emissions in feedback form. In a linearised case the carbon price is deterministic and the default probability is explicit. Unlike a reward-penalty scheme, the trading system costs nothing while the cap is slack and forces firms to meet it exactly once it binds. Calibrated to 35 European power producers, the model reproduces the average EU allowance price over 2018–2024."
