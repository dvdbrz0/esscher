  "Speaking in New York on 23 November, John Williams, president of the Federal Reserve Bank of New York, said the Fed must act over time to bring inflation back to its 2% target and that there is still work to do. He did not name a timetable but said he would not exclude moving at consecutive meetings if needed. The comments suggested he favours a rise at the meeting of 8 and 9 December, when markets expect the target range to go up by a quarter point from 3.75–4.00%.",
  "Williams expects inflation to end 2026 at 3.5%, fall to just above 2% next year and reach the target in 2028, with risks skewed upwards and energy prices elevated. He sees growth of 2.75% in both 2026 and 2027 and unemployment, now 4.1%, near 4% next year. He said the stock market’s swings had not dimmed his view of the economy."
