  "The authors propose OLIVIA, a protocol that rules out look-ahead, memorisation and selection: point-in-time inputs with company names masked, selection on validation data only, an analysis plan frozen by cryptographic hash and a test period unblinded once. They apply it to models reading news about S&P 500 stocks.",
  "A fine-tuned open-weights model replicated on two validation quarters, with daily information coefficients of 0.055 and 0.065, but fell to 0.020 with a t-statistic of 1.7 on the January to July 2026 test. Commercial models’ readings match the overnight move to the open, not the move after it, so the news is priced before a trader can act. No pre-registered strategy survived costs and a deflated Sharpe ratio, and masking hid names but not companies, against a 28% baseline."
