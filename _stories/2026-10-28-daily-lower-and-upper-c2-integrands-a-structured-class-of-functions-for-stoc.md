  "The authors extend lower-\\(C^2\\) functions, pointwise maxima of twice continuously differentiable functions over a compact index set, to normal integrands, where the index set is random and the Hessians are integrable and bounded. Their main result is that such integrands satisfy a local sufficient ascent property almost surely; equivalently, they differ from a convex normal integrand by a random quadratic. Upper-\\(C^2\\) integrands follow by a change of sign and appear as second-stage value functions of stochastic programs with compact recourse.",
  "For this class, integrability of the function values, of the local Lipschitz moduli and of the minimum-norm Clarke subgradients are shown to be equivalent. The paper places smooth integrands, difference-of-convex integrands, Moreau envelopes and several compositions in the framework, and shows that the variational formulation of conditional value-at-risk fits within it, giving structure for stochastic methods on nonconvex risk objectives."
