 "body": [
  "Gatheral conjectured in 2005 that among all diffusions consistent with the prices of European options of every strike and maturity, local volatility dynamics give the lowest value of a volatility option. Counterexamples by Beiglböck and coauthors in 2011 showed the statement false, but they were not models used in practice.",
  "Alòs, Burés and Lucic give general sufficient conditions, based on asymptotics of integrated variance derivatives and Wiener chaos expansions, for a stochastic volatility model to refute the conjecture. A general class of rough volatility models qualifies, and for Markovian variance processes the conjecture fails under a relation between the coefficients of the variance equation. The paper works through Heston and SABR examples."
