  "Take independent centred random vectors \\(X_1, \\dots, X_n\\) with finite fourth moments and their normalised sum \\(W_n = n^{-1/2} \\sum_{i} X_i\\). The paper bounds the Wasserstein distance between \\(W_n\\) and a Gaussian with the same covariance, with no dependence on the dimension. The coupling is strong: a single coupling controls the error of every projection onto a subset of the eigen-coordinates simultaneously.",
  "The proof combines stochastic localisation with a Hermite expansion under a Gaussian prior and exchangeable pairs. It proves a recent conjecture and improves the constant on the fourth-moment term from \\(2\\) to \\(197/108\\). The bound extends to real separable Hilbert spaces, where for independent identically distributed observations the \\(L^2\\) prediction error is of order \\(O(n^{-1/2})\\), with no dimension factor and no condition on how fast the covariance eigenvalues decay. An \\(\\ell^2\\) example shows that the coordinate-wise rate \\(n^{-1/2}\\) is sharp."
