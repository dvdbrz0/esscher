Anthropic’s particle, built from a phage protein it calls L05 and made in E. coli, has 96 copies and a mass of about 9.5 megadaltons. It measures roughly 54 by 45 nanometres and encloses about 25% more volume than the mammalian form.",
  "Under human direction, Claude searched about 15bn predicted proteins, designed DNA constructs, helped cut purification from three days to about 90 minutes and ran the cryo-electron microscopy processing. A first model took 34 hours. Copying two wall segments enlarged the enclosed volume by about 20%.",
  "Anthropic also lists the errors. A protocol with the wrong additive cost two weeks, and a skipped step produced a map blended with its mirror image. It has not tested whether these vaults form in their natural hosts. The manuscript has been submitted to bioRxiv."
