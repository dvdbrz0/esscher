  "Self-normalised sums, which divide by the sample standard deviation rather than a known one, are what practitioners actually compute, for example in simultaneous \\(t\\)-tests over many coordinates. The paper studies Gaussian approximation over all coordinate rectangles for such sums of independent, identically distributed centred vectors in dimension \\(d\\), with standardised third absolute moments bounded by a constant \\(K > 1\\).",
  "Under arbitrary dependence between coordinates, uniform approximation holds exactly when \\(d = o(\\sqrt{n})\\); if off-diagonal correlations satisfy \\(R_{jk} \\le 1 - a\\) for a fixed gap \\(a\\), the condition becomes \\(\\log d = o(n^{1/3})\\). With \\(q \\ge 3\\) marginal moments the sharp error order is \\(1 \\wedge \\{d/n^{(q-2)/2}\\}^{1/q}\\). The proof replaces observations one at a time while keeping their effect on numerator and denominator in one expansion."
