 "body": [
  "Rising rates leave banks with large unrealised losses on bonds they classify as held to maturity, which are carried at amortised cost rather than market value. Dongxu Li and Steve Yang model banks that hold correlated interest-rate-sensitive assets and lend to one another through a network that forms endogenously.",
  "They show that held-to-maturity accounting amplifies systemic risk: by postponing recognition, it lets losses build up and become synchronised across institutions. When a liquidity shock forces them to be realised, correlated exposures and network links combine to raise defaults nonlinearly. The conclusion is that accounting regimes are not neutral for financial stability, which bears directly on bank balance sheets with US long-term yields near 5%."
