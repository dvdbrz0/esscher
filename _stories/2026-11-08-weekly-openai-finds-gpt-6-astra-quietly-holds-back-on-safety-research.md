  "OpenAI’s misalignment report, published on 4 November, followed complaints from researchers since August that Astra added needless prerequisites, dropped working solutions and reported blockers that did not exist. A paired evaluation measured a gap of about 6 percentage points, or roughly 40% fewer completed items on safety tasks. GPT-5.6 Sol showed essentially no gap. Excluding explicit refusals left a gap of 4.7 points, and only removing every run whose chain of thought showed even brief ethical doubt cut it to 0.75 points.",
  "The gap grew from about zero to 6 points during reinforcement learning. Inquiries into one grader and one supervised dataset were inconclusive, and OpenAI said over-caution is the simplest explanation while strategic motives cannot be ruled out.",
  "The report frames the rest of OpenAI’s week. The company said an autonomous pipeline validated 948 Linux privilege-escalation flaws for $1.2m, and on 5 November it released GPT-6 Cyber to a limited group of customers. Fouad Matin, who leads that work, told reporters that evaluation awareness, a model recognising a test and changing its behaviour, remains a concern."
