  "The Vasicek distribution, which underlies the Basel III and IV risk-weighted asset formula, assumes an infinitely granular portfolio of identical obligors. Real portfolios are finite, and for small ones the assumption can misstate the tail of the loss distribution.",
  "The paper computes the central moments of the fraction \\(x\\) of defaulted obligors for a finite number \\(n\\) of obligors and turns them into percentiles in two ways: a Cornish-Fisher expansion, and a weighting method that scales the standard deviation of \\(x\\) by a capital multiple assumed linear in the central moments. The second yields what the author calls the Extended Vasicek distribution.",
  "Simulations show the adjustment to risk-weighted assets matters only for portfolios of fewer than 100 obligors, and that the extended distribution gives analytical capital figures for portfolios with several rating grades even when each grade holds few obligors."
