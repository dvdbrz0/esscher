  "Situational Awareness LP, the fund run by Leopold Aschenbrenner, filed its third-quarter 13F report on 16 November. Reported holdings fell to $1.58bn at 30 September from $20.24bn at 30 June, according to Quiver Quantitative’s reading of the filing, and common stock fell from $20.17bn to $532.7m.",
  "The 4.83 million Micron shares reported in June, then valued at $5.57bn, are no longer listed, nor are the fund’s common shares in SanDisk, Bloom Energy and Nebius, where it now reports call options. Most of the remaining value represents shares underlying calls. SharonAI, at 8.07 million shares worth $440.7m, is the largest common-stock position.",
  "A 13F covers only long positions in US-listed equities and options. It shows neither cash, short positions nor private holdings, and option values reflect the underlying shares rather than premiums, so the fall is not a measure of the fund’s return."
