  "On the eve of Anthropic’s listing, the Center for AI Safety (CAIS) and others published the MisAnthropic Statement.   "Signatories named by CAIS include Steven Pinker, the economist Oliver Hart, Judea Pearl, Lawrence Lessig, Thomas Wolf of Hugging Face, Max Tegmark, the Fields medallists Peter Scholze, James Maynard and David Mumford, and Guillaume Verdon, founder of the accelerationist movement. CAIS cited Anthropic’s revision of its Responsible Scaling Policy in February, which dropped its unilateral commitments to pause, and its lobbying against several state AI bills.",
  "The statement brings together groups that usually disagree, from AI safety researchers and open-source advocates to accelerationists. Dan Hendrycks, executive director of CAIS, said the company could not credibly claim to be safe while weakening its earlier commitments."
