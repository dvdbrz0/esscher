  "Researchers at ETH Zurich and Sapienza University of Rome built an agentic system that represents each economic theory paper as a claim graph, in which model assumptions and intermediate lemmas support the headline results. In a random sample of 190 papers published since 1995 in four leading journals, removing the most consequential starting input leaves on average 91% of weighted headline and major claims unsupported.",
  "Benchmarked against published corrections to 100 papers, the system fully recovered 79 of 168 documented errors, or 47%, and 69% when partial matches count. The graphs then measure what a correction costs: among corrected papers the median share of conclusions an error touches is 28%, but the median loss of economic content after the smallest valid repair is 4%, and 2% in the random sample.",
