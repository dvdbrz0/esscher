 "body": [
  "Classical bands for an unknown distribution function, such as those from the Dvoretzky–Kiefer–Wolfowitz inequality, assume a fixed sample size and independent data. This paper builds bands that remain valid uniformly over time, so they can be monitored continuously and stopped at any moment, and over every point of the domain.",
  "The authors first treat the time-averaged conditional distribution function of a stream in which each observation may depend on the past, and show that the width of the band is governed by the depth of a binary search tree. They then present this as an instance of a general framework for any indexed family of time-averaged distribution functions, and apply it to importance-weighted, right-censored and multivariate distribution functions."
