Follow Esscher in a feed reader

Every Daily Brief and Weekly Review, delivered to the app you already use to follow websites.

A feed reader gathers new articles from the sites you choose into one list, without an account on those sites and without email. Esscher publishes each edition in a feed that any reader can follow. Add this address to your reader:

{{ '/feed.xml' | absolute_url }}

With a reader you already use

Follow in Feedly or follow in Inoreader. In NetNewsWire, Reeder or Thunderbird, add a new feed and paste the address above.

What you get

One entry per edition: the lines of the day and every headline, each linked to its full story on the site. New editions appear within minutes of going online, at 05:00 Bucharest time on weekdays and on Sunday mornings.

Opening the address itself in a browser shows the raw feed, written for reader apps rather than for people.
