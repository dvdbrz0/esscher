**Papers**

- [Proving and Improving the Platonic Representation Hypothesis with JEPAs](https://esscher.com/stories/2026-11-02-daily-proving-and-improving-the-platonic-representation-hypothesis-with-jepa/): JEPAs trained separately on any number of modalities learn the same representation up to one rotation when positive pairs are two views of one scene. Next-token prediction is shown to be a case with bad views.
- [Closing the Log Gap: Minimax-Optimal Regret for Episodic RL](https://esscher.com/stories/2026-11-02-daily-closing-the-log-gap-minimax-optimal-regret-for-episodic-rl/): A variant of UCBVI reaches expected regret of order √H³SAK for large K, matching the minimax lower bound with no extra polylogarithmic factors for the first time.
- [There Is No Free Lunch in RLVR: Every pass@1 Gain Has a pass@k Price](https://esscher.com/stories/2026-11-02-daily-there-is-no-free-lunch-in-rlvr-every-pass-1-gain-has-a-pass-k-price/): Modelling reinforcement learning as an exponential tilt of a base policy, the paper proves that first-try gains cost success on other prompts and force a crossover at large sampling budgets k.
- [Comparing tail forecasts: the number of exceedances is the sample size](https://esscher.com/stories/2026-11-02-daily-comparing-tail-forecasts-the-number-of-exceedances-is-the-sample-size/): When two tail forecasts are compared by a consistent score, exceedances are the effective sample size. With few of them, the Diebold-Mariano test favours the less conservative forecaster.
