**The maths behind the news**

- [Why the winner of a leaderboard is overrated, and by how much](https://esscher.com/stories/2026-11-01-weekly-why-the-winner-of-a-leaderboard-is-overrated-and-by-how-much/): When the best of M noisy but unbiased scores is reported, its expected value is too high. **Read this week**

- Anytime Valid Confidence Bands for Distribution Functions: Confidence bands for the distribution function of a data stream that hold at all times and all points at once, even when each observation depends on the past: a sequential, martingale counterpart of the Dvoretzky-Kiefer-Wolfowitz inequality, useful wherever a model is monitored continuously.
- The Cost of an Unknown Horizon for the Last Iterate of Clipped SGD under Heavy-Tailed Noise: The optimal time-uniform rate for the last iterate of clipped SGD under noise with a bounded central pth moment is Õ(t^(−βₚ)) with βₚ = 2(p−1)/(3p−1), strictly worse than O(T^(−αₚ)), αₚ = (p−1)/p, when the horizon is known; a clean example of what data-dependent stopping costs.
