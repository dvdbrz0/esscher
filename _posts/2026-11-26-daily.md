1. Google posted six papers written entirely by its Cogentic system, including an unconditional factoring algorithm running in time L_N[1/3, 105], a theoretical result that leaves RSA as it was in practice.
2. Wall Street recovered most of a steep morning sell-off before Thanksgiving, with the Nasdaq closing 0.6% lower after falling as much as 4.7%, as core PCE inflation rose to 3.3%.
3. The cost of insuring the debt of AI companies reached records, with CoreWeave’s five-year credit default swap spread at 1,314 basis points.

**Must-read**

- [Google’s Cogentic proves that integers can be factored in time L[1/3] unconditionally](https://esscher.com/stories/2026-11-26-daily-googles-cogentic-proves-that-integers-can-be-factored-in-time-l-1-3-un/): Google researchers posted six papers produced by an agentic system built on Gemini 4 from a single prompt, led by a factoring algorithm with expected running time L_N[1/3, 105] that relies on no unproven hypotheses.
