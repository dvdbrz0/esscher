**What researchers are saying**

- [Alpöge says the 250,000-page figure for his Riemann proof was a joke](https://esscher.com/stories/2026-11-24-daily-alpoge-says-the-250-000-page-figure-for-his-riemann-proof-was-a-joke/): Alpöge wrote that the page count attached to his Claude-assisted Lean proof of the Riemann hypothesis was a joke and that the Lean code is full of garbage, although it compiles and passes the kernel.
- [Chollet says intelligent systems adapt on exactly two timescales](https://esscher.com/stories/2026-11-24-daily-chollet-says-intelligent-systems-adapt-on-exactly-two-timescales/): Chollet argued that every intelligent system he has seen adapts in two ways, by modelling a situation on the fly and by distilling reusable abstractions from long experience, and that AI had only the second until late 2024.
- [LeCun says video prediction needed joint embedding rather than generation](https://esscher.com/stories/2026-11-24-daily-lecun-says-video-prediction-needed-joint-embedding-rather-than-generat/): LeCun argued that progress in predicting video came only after abandoning purely generative models for joint embedding predictive architectures, with the main uses in robotics and industrial process control.

**Papers**

- [Sharp Last-Iterate Bounds for the Stochastic subGradient Method](https://esscher.com/stories/2026-11-24-daily-sharp-last-iterate-bounds-for-the-stochastic-subgradient-method/): The worst-case expected error of the last iterate of constant-stepsize projected stochastic subgradient descent on convex Lipschitz objectives is pinned down up to universal constants, and it depends on the dimension.
- [A sharp dimension barrier for uniform Gaussian approximations](https://esscher.com/stories/2026-11-24-daily-a-sharp-dimension-barrier-for-uniform-gaussian-approximations/): For n independent vectors in ℝᵈ with bounded m-th coordinate moments, a Gaussian approximation uniform over hyperrectangles requires log d = o(n^((m−2)/(3m−2))), and this is sharp for m ∈ (2, 4].
- [Optimal Linear Regression Without a Variance](https://esscher.com/stories/2026-11-24-daily-optimal-linear-regression-without-a-variance/): A residual-pruning reduction turns any sub-Gaussian-rate regression method into one that is minimax optimal when the noise has only a finite α-moment for an unknown α ∈ (1, 2).
- [Held-to-Maturity Accounting: Hidden Losses and Systemic Fragility](https://esscher.com/stories/2026-11-24-daily-held-to-maturity-accounting-hidden-losses-and-systemic-fragility/): In a model of banks with correlated rate-sensitive assets and an endogenous interbank network, held-to-maturity accounting delays loss recognition, synchronises losses and produces nonlinear jumps in defaults under liquidity stress.
